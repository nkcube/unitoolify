How it works

Chrome and Edge never shipped a way to put separators on the bookmarks bar, so this page is the workaround: the button above is an ordinary bookmark that happens to render as a thin line. Drag it onto the bar and you are done — no extension, no sync service, nothing to install.

Vertical or horizontal. The vertical separator is a bookmark with an empty title (so it takes up almost no room), while the horizontal one uses a run of box-drawing characters as its title. Pick whichever reads better against your existing bookmarks.

Note for Edge users: Edge refuses to add duplicate bookmarks, so every time you press or drag a button it gets a fresh random fragment in its URL. That is why you can add as many separators as you want without hitting a duplicate wall.

A separator is just a bookmark, so it syncs through your browser account and survives restarts. Two or three lines are usually enough to keep a busy bar organised.

Removing one

Right-click the separator on your bookmarks bar and choose Delete. There is no state stored anywhere else — this page only generates the bookmark, it does not remember or sync anything you add.
