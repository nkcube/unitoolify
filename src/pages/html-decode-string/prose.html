About HTML Decoding

The opposite of the encoder: &lt; goes back to <, &amp; back to &, and so on. Useful whenever you're staring at escaped content and want to see what it actually says.

Almost everything exported from a CMS, wiki or RSS feed comes out entity-encoded, and email templates are full of the stuff. Paste it in here and you get the readable original back, whether the entities are named (&amp;) or numeric (&#39;).

Decoding happens locally in your browser — no data leaves your machine.
