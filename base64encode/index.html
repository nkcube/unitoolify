About Base64 Encoding

Welcome to Unitoolify's Base64 encoder. This tool allows you to quickly transform your text or binary files into Base64 encoded format entirely within your browser. Your data is never sent to our servers, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Base64 is widely used on the web to transmit binary data over text-based protocols (like email via MIME, or embedded images in CSS/HTML). It converts binary data into a string of ASCII characters using a 64-character alphabet.

Use the advanced options to tailor the output to your specific needs, such as generating a URL-safe Base64 string that replaces the standard "+" and "/" characters with "-" and "_" respectively, and omits padding "=" signs.
