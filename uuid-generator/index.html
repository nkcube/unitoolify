About UUID Generator

Welcome to Unitoolify's UUID Generator. A UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit label used for information in computer systems. This tool generates standard Version 1 (timestamp) and Version 4 (random) UUIDs.

Secure and Private: We use the standard open-source library (`uuid`) to generate these identifiers securely within your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers, ensuring your generated UUIDs remain entirely private.

You can generate up to 10,000 UUIDs at once, choose to output them in uppercase, and optionally strip out the hyphen separators if your database or system requires a compact format.
